Questions

FAQ

How do I pay? +
I accept M-Pesa and bank transfer. Payment is split 50% upfront to begin and 50% on delivery of the final site.
How long does a website take? +
A starter single-page site takes about 5 days. A standard multi-page site takes 7–14 days depending on content and complexity. I'll give you an exact timeline in your quote.
Do I need to provide photos and text? +
Yes — you provide your logo, photos, and the text content. If you don't have photos, I can use high-quality free images from Unsplash that match your brand. I can also help write short copy if needed.
Can I update the website myself after it's done? +
Yes! Since I use plain HTML and CSS, you can edit text and images directly in VS Code. I'll walk you through how if needed. More complex changes — just contact me.
What if I need changes after the site is live? +
You get 30 days of free support after launch. After that, small updates are charged at KES 500–1,000 depending on the task.
Do you offer a domain name? +
I don't sell domains directly but I'll help you buy one from a registrar like GoDaddy or Safaricom and connect it to your site — it usually costs about KES 1,000–2,000 per year.
