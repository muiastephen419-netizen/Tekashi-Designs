How I work

My Process

From first message to live website — here's exactly how I work with clients to deliver beautiful, results-driven websites on time.

Step by step

How we build together

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Initial Consultation

    We start with a free chat — WhatsApp, call or in-person in Nairobi. I ask about your business, target audience, competitors, and what you need the website to achieve. No jargon, no pressure.

  2. 02

    Quote

    Proposal & Agreement

    I send you a clear quote with the project scope, timeline, and price. Once you're happy, we sign a simple agreement and you pay a 50% deposit to kick things off.

  3. 03

    Design

    Design & Structure

    I plan the pages, layout, colours, and structure based on your brand and goals. You review and approve the direction before I write a single line of code.

  4. 04

    Development

    Build & Code

    I build your site in clean HTML, CSS & JavaScript — pixel-perfect, fully responsive on mobile, fast-loading, and optimised for search engines. You get progress updates throughout.

  5. 05

    Review

    Review & Revisions

    I share the finished site for your review. You get up to 3 rounds of revisions to make sure every detail is exactly right before we go live.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Go Live & Handover

    Your site goes live! I handle GitHub Pages deployment, domain linking, and hand over all your files. You pay the remaining 50% and get 30 days of free support included.
